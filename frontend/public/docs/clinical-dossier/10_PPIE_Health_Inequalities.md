# SEWA AI — Patient & Public Involvement, Engagement and Health Inequalities

**Document Ref:** SEWA-AI-DOC-10 · **Version:** 1.0 · **July 2026**
**Standards:** INVOLVE / NIHR PPIE principles; NHS Equality Duty; DPIA-001 equity clauses

---

## 1. Current PPIE Status (Honest)

Formal PPIE activities are **yet to commence**. SEWA AI is at TRL 5 and now transitions to clinical
validation where PPIE becomes essential. This document states the **planned** PPIE programme; it is
not evidence of completed engagement.

---

## 2. Planned PPIE Activities

| Phase | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 2026 | Establish PPIE advisory group (6–8 patient reps with lived experience of diagnostic delay) | Terms of reference |
| Q4 2026 | Patient focus groups on AI acceptability, explainability, alert fatigue | Co-designed patient info |
| Q1 2027 | Co-design shadow-trial materials; plain-language summaries | Patient-facing docs |
| Q2 2027+ | Ongoing surveys (post-alert); quarterly advisory meetings | Continuous feedback |
| Q3 2027 | Africa PPIE via AMREF + local groups | Localised materials |

Partners to approach: Stroke Association, British Lung Foundation, TB Alert, INVOLVE, NIHR PPIE centres.

## 3. How PPIE Shapes the Technology

- Study documentation and patient information materials.
- Acceptability / transparency / explainability preferences.
- Research priorities and patient-relevant outcome measures.
- Accessibility, inclusivity and equality throughout development.
