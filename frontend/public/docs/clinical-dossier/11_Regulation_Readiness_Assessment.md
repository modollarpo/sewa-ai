# SEWA AI — Regulation-Readiness Assessment

**Document Ref:** SEWA-AI-DOC-11 · **Version:** 1.0 · **July 2026**
**Question addressed:** *Is the clinical study / research evidence sufficient for SEWA AI to be regulated (UKCA Class IIb / EU MDR CE)?*

---

## 1. Bottom Line

**No — not yet.** The clinical evidence generated to date (internal engineering testing, TRL 5) is
**insufficient** to obtain UKCA Class IIb or EU MDR CE marking. However, the **research programme
described in this dossier (SEWA-AI-DOC-02/03), if executed as designed with an NHS Chief
Investigator, an independent statistician, REC/HRA/MHRA approvals, and a positive outcome, would
provide the clinical evidence required** to support a conformity assessment. Certification then also
requires a functioning ISO 13485 QMS, completed technical documentation, and a successful UK
Approved Body / EU Notified Body audit.

In short: the *study design* is sufficient and regulation-ready; the *evidence* is not yet generated.

### Gate A — Engineering / analytical validity
- **Status:** Not met — internal engineering runs only; no analytical validity claimed.
- **Caveat:** internal engineering figures are not reported as validation results and must be
  reproduced independently before any analytical-validity claim.

### Gate B — Independent clinical validation (the decisive gap)
- **Status:** **Not met.** No NHS prospective/retrospective validation completed.
- **Remedy:** Execute SEWA-AI-DOC-02 with locked SAP (DOC-03). This is the single most important
  evidence gap.

### Gate C — Clinical utility & economic
- **Status:** Not met. Door-to-alert and health-economic modelling pending.
- **Remedy:** Prospective workflow endpoint + NIHR EME health-economic evaluation.

### Gate D — Quality & conformity
- **Status:** Partially met (DCB0129, ISO 14971/62304/62366 files drafted). ISO 13485 QMS and
  Approved Body audit outstanding.

## 4. Critical Blockers (Must Resolve Before Certification)

1. **Chief Investigator** — NHS/University consultant radiologist (HRA mandatory). Without this, no
   REC approval, no study, no CER.
2. **Independent statistician** — to lock the SAP before unblinding.
3. **REC / HRA / MHRA approvals** — IRAS 372596 in progress.
4. **ISO 13485 QMS** — must be operational before the audit.
5. **Successful study outcome** — the device must meet its pre-specified endpoints.

---

## 5. Is the Clinical *Study Design* "Enough"?

For regulation, the *design* must be: prospective or retrospective vs. a qualified reference
standard, multi-centre, pre-specified, independent, and reported to TRIPOD-AI/DECIDE-AI. The
SEWA-AI-DOC-02/03 design **meets these methodological expectations**. So the study, once run, will be
**fit for purpose** to support the Clinical Evaluation Report. The current absence is execution, not
design.

## 7. Honest Limitations & Risks

- Internal performance may **not** reproduce in independent cohorts (optimism bias).
- Single sponsor-CI conflict of interest (mitigated by independent oversight + TRE arm's-length).
- Future modules (Pathology/Mammography/Retinal) are **excluded** and carry no claims (DOC-09).
- If the prospective study fails to meet endpoints, certification is not achievable until redesign.

## 8. Conclusion

SEWA AI has a **regulation-ready design and a credible, phased evidence plan**, and has completed the
NHS clinical-safety (DCB0129) and foundational standards work. It is **not yet regulatable** because
the independent clinical validation, QMS and conformity-assessment steps remain outstanding. Executing
the prospective clinical investigation described here — with the critical CI appointment — is the
decisive action that converts "promising TRL 5 platform" into an evidence-supported Class IIb SaMD
candidate for UKCA/CE marking.
