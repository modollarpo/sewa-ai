# SEWA AI — NICE Evidence Standards Alignment

**Document Ref:** SEWA-AI-DOC-08 · **Version:** 1.0 · **July 2026**
**Reference:** NICE Evidence Standards Framework for digital health technologies; NICE DG / MIB AI medical device guidance

---

## 1. NICE Evidence Standards Framework — Tiers

NICE sets evidence tiers by the level of clinical risk and decision support. SEWA AI, as a
**diagnostic/decision-support AI that informs management of serious conditions**, maps to the
**highest evidence tier** ( Tier 3-equivalent: clinical pathway impact + robust analytical & clinical
validation + economic evaluation).

---

## 2. Standards Mapping

| NICE standard | Requirement | SEWA AI status |
|---|---|---|
| Analytical validity | Reproducible, accurate algorithm performance | Internal engineering testing run; **no clinical results claimed — independent validation required** |
| Diagnostic accuracy | Sensitivity/specificity vs. reference standard | Prospective study designed (SEWA-AI-DOC-02) |
| Clinical utility | Impact on outcomes / workflow | Door-to-alert endpoint; NHS pilot planned |
| Economic evidence | Cost-effectiveness for NHS | Health-economic evaluation planned (NIHR EME) |
| Real-world validation | Prospective, multi-site | 4 Trust proposals submitted |
| Safety / risk | DCB0129 + ISO 14971 | Complete / in progress |
| Usability | IEC 62366-1 | In progress |
| Equity | Subgroup performance | SOP-AI-005 equity audit |

---

## 3. Evidence Gaps Against NICE (honest assessment)

- **Independent clinical accuracy** not yet demonstrated in NHS cohorts — the prospective study (SEWA-AI-DOC-02) is the remedy.
- **Clinical utility** currently inferred from engineering time-to-triage modelling; needs prospective confirmation.
- **Economic evaluation** not yet completed; required for NICE/MIB adoption.
- **Real-world bias monitoring** requires the live deployment phase.

---

## 4. Pathway to NICE Recommendation

1. Complete prospective validation (SEWA-AI-DOC-02) with independent statistician.
2. Generate Clinical Evaluation Report + health-economic model.
3. Engage NICE (MIB or DG) post-certification for adoption guidance.
4. Continuous PMS feeds re-assessment.

---

## 5. Reporting Standards

Studies will be reported per **TRIPOD-AI** (prediction model) and **DECIDE-AI** (early-stage AI
clinical evaluation), and registered on ISRCTN.
