# SEWA AI — Future Services Pipeline (PLANNED, NOT YET DEPLOYED)

**Document Ref:** SEWA-AI-DOC-09 · **Version:** 1.0 · July 2026
**Regulatory status of these modules: PLANNED — out of scope of current Class IIb submission and current clinical investigation.**

---

## 1. Purpose of This Document

The SEWA AI platform is architected as a **modular, multi-specialty clinical intelligence platform**.
The initial regulated product is **Radiology AI** (thoracic/acute imaging triage), which is the sole
subject of the clinical investigation (SEWA-AI-DOC-02) and the Class IIb conformity assessment
(SEWA-AI-DOC-01).

This document records three **intended future modules** — **Pathology AI, Mammography AI and Retinal
AI** — so that stakeholders understand the roadmap. It is explicit that:

> **These modules are NOT deployed, NOT clinically validated, NOT CE/UKCA marked, and carry NO
> current clinical performance claims.** They are described here only as future development
> intentions and are excluded from the current regulatory submission and clinical study.**

## 3. Intended Future Modules (Roadmap Only)

### 3.1 Pathology AI (Planned — Year 4+)
- **Intended future use:** decision-support for histopathology / microscopy triage.
- **Status:** not developed; no data, no model, no validation.
- **Regulatory:** would require its own intended-use definition, standalone risk file (ISO 14971),
  software lifecycle (IEC 62304) and clinical investigation before any claim.

### 3.2 Mammography AI (Planned — Year 5+)
- **Intended future use:** breast-imaging triage / lesion flagging.
- **Status:** not developed; no data, no model, no validation.
- **Regulatory:** distinct breast-screening regulatory considerations (e.g., EU MDR Annex XVI /
  national screening governance); separate submission.

### 3.3 Retinal AI (Planned — Year 5+)
- **Intended future use:** fundus / OCT screening decision-support.
- **Status:** not developed; no data, no model, no validation.
- **Regulatory:** standalone intended use; separate clinical investigation and conformity assessment.

---

## 4. Governance of Future Modules

- Each future module will **not** be marketed or deployed until it has its own locked intended-use
  statement, independent clinical validation, completed risk and usability files, and applicable
  UKCA/CE marking.
- Until then, any reference to Pathology / Mammography / Retinal AI in marketing or publications will
  be explicitly labelled **"planned future development — not yet available."**

---

## 5. Statement of Non-Deception

To avoid any misrepresentation to regulators, clinicians or patients: **SEWA AI's current clinical
evidence and regulatory dossier pertain exclusively to Radiology AI (thoracic/acute triage).** No
performance, safety or clinical claim is made for Pathology, Mammography or Retinal AI at this time.
