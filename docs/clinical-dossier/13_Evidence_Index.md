## How to read this index
- **Design vs. evidence:** Documents 02/03/11 confirm the clinical investigation is
  *designed and powered*; they are not clinical results. Document 12 confirms the
  software *runs*; it is not performance evidence.
- **Future modules:** Document 09 is explicitly a pipeline plan (Pathology,
  Mammography, Retinal are PLANNED, not in current scope) — consistent with the
  form's modular-roadmap narrative.
- **Out of dossier scope:** Environmental sustainability (form §12) and commercial
  pricing are addressed in the form narrative itself; they are strategic, not
  evidenced by a dedicated dossier document.
