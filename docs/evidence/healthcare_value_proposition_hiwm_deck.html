1 SCENE SETTING
SEWA XAI
What it is and where it fits
The innovation
A containerised, modular clinical intelligence platform. Its first module triages non-contrast head CT for intracranial haemorrhage (ICH) using CPU-optimised ONNX INT8 inference, with uncertainty quantification and HiResCAM explainability.
Where in the system
Emergency departments, acute receiving units, hyper-acute stroke units (HASU) and teleradiology reading workflows in secondary and tertiary acute NHS trusts.
Who it is relevant to
Emergency physicians, consultant radiologists, reporting radiographers, stroke physicians, and acute care pathway coordinators.
Pathway it supports
Pre-read prioritisation and worklist sorting for acute head CT. It supports the existing clinical pathway and does not replace diagnostic reporting. Radiologist sign-off is mandatory on 100% of scans.

2 UNMET NEED
SEWA XAI
Urgent scans wait in line
THE CAUSE
Reporting backlogs, workforce shortages and rising emergency CT volumes delay detection of time-critical findings such as intracranial haemorrhage.
Today's worklist is first-in, first-out
Standard PACS worklists do not re-order studies by clinical urgency before they are opened. During peak hours a critical head CT can sit unread behind routine studies.
Existing AI triage has gaps
Some commercial tools need costly dedicated GPUs (£30k–£50k/node), give no transparent uncertainty, or operate as black boxes that fit poorly with sovereign data residency and NHS safety standards (DSPT, DCB0129).

3 CONSEQUENCE
SEWA XAI
What the delay costs
Patient and staff experience
Later identification of acute ICH raises clinical risk in stroke and trauma pathways, and intensifies burnout among emergency and radiology clinicians.
Clinical outcomes
Delayed intervention in acute ICH is linked to haematoma expansion, higher 30-day mortality (35–45%), and long-term functional dependency.
Resources and service
Emergency department crowding, 4-hour target breaches, bottlenecked out-of-hours reporting and heavy reliance on locum/teleradiology cover.
Sourced NHS Baseline Metrics (NHS Digital DID, RCR Census & GIRFT):
• Emergency CT Demand: Over 2.5M emergency head CT scans per year in NHS England, growing at ~8% annually.
• Radiology Workforce Deficit: 29% UK consultant shortfall (RCR 2023/24 Census); West Midlands trusts average 26% vacancy.
• Turnaround Lag: Out-of-hours routine queue delay averages 120–240+ minutes; locum/teleradiology costs exceed £285M annually in England.

Safety and governance by design
Clinical safety (DCB0129)
Hazard log (CRM-001) and clinical safety case maintained for each deployment under named CSO.
Data protection (DSPT)
Sovereign UK South Azure or fully offline edge operation. Compliant with NHS DSPT and Caldicott principles.
