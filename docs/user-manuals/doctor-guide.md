# Doctor User Guide

## SEWA AI — Clinician/Doctor Guide
---

## 1. Overview
As a Doctor, you receive critical alerts, view reports, manage patients and referrals!
---

## 2. Dashboard
The Doctor Dashboard has:
- Critical Findings List (urgent cases at top)
- Patient List
- Appointment Calendar
- Notifications Panel

---

## 3. Receiving Alerts
When there's a critical finding (like ICH, massive PE, etc.)
- Push notification on mobile
- In-app banner flash in red
- Sound and vibration
- Optional SMS backup (for low connectivity regions)

---

## 4. Viewing Reports
- Tap an alert or click a patient in list
- See:
  - AI findings summary + confidence
  - Grad-CAM overlay
  - Signed radiology report (if ready)
  - Patient history
---

## 5. Patient Portal
Your patients can log in and:
- View their scans and signed reports
- Message the care team
- Manage consent preferences
---

## 6. Referrals & Second Opinions
- Request referrals from within SEWA
- Use the SEWA Referral Network for specialist opinions from anywhere
---

## 7. Settings
- Manage your profile
- Notification preferences
- MFA settings
---
