## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the content marketing strategy for SEWA AI. It defines the audience, platforms, content pillars, post sequences, publishing rhythm, and scheduling logic needed to attract readers, build trust, and drive users to the SEWA AI platform.

The strategy is built on one premise: **the market does not have a trust-deficit because of awareness — it has a trust-deficit because of evidence.** SEWA's content must educate first, differentiate second, and convert third.

## 5. Content Sequences

Posts are grouped into **sequences** — ordered blocks that tell a connected story before moving to the next topic.

### Sequence 1: SEWA Feature Education (Weeks 1–4) — FIRST
Teach users and readers what SEWA AI is and what it does. Build foundational understanding before differentiation.
- 7 posts covering platform overview, Uncertainty Engine, metacognition/hallucination gate, safety architecture, edge/offline, non-bypassable sign-off, integrated workflow.

### Sequence 2: Clinical AI Literacy (Weeks 5–6)
Educate the broader market on concepts SEWA embodies (uncertainty quantification, trust architecture, hallucination in clinical AI).
- 3 posts — positions SEWA as thought leader, not just product.

### Sequence 3: NHS & Regulatory Context (Weeks 7–9)
G-Cloud 14, MHRA reforms, DCB0129/0160, NICE EVA, UKCA Class IIb pathway.
- 3 posts — procurement and regulatory credibility.

### Sequence 4: Market & Competitive Positioning (Weeks 10–11)
Competitive landscape, evidence bar (Brainomix, DeepHealth), trustworthy-AI narrative.

### Sequence 5: Vision, Evidence Pathway & Partnerships (Weeks 12–14)
Shadow trial → NIHR EME → UKCA Class IIb, PPIE, funding, 5-year vision.
- 3 posts — converts awareness into research/partnership interest.

## 6. Publishing Rhythm & Scheduling Logic

- **Frequency**: 3 posts/week (one per weekday: Tue, Thu, Sat) — sustainable cadence.
- **Stagger dates**: Each post has a unique `publishDate` in the content calendar. Publish posts where `status = "scheduled"` and `publishDate <= today`.
- **Why staggered**: A batch of posts published on the same day dilutes reach, signals bulk-production, and reduces algorithm favour. Staggering sustains presence and each post gets its own engagement window.
- **Publishing window**: 08:00–10:00 GMT (peak LinkedIn/NHS professional activity).

## 8. Content Creation Rules (per post)

1. Every factual claim is classified (verified public fact / SEWA internal / unvalidated claim / external evidence) with dated source.
2. Never present internal engineering results as clinical evidence.
3. Never overclaim regulatory status or NHS adoption.
4. Lead with the problem/need, then introduce SEWA.
5. Include a clear CTA: read the full report, book a demo, join the research programme, or sign up for the newsletter.
6. Target 1,200–1,800 words for full long-form articles; 800–1,000 words for LinkedIn posts.
7. Every post gets a cover image and a featured image for social sharing.

## 9. Funnel Logic (how posts get users to the platform)

```
LinkedIn / Twitter post (awareness)
  └─→ CTA: "See how it works" / "Read the full report" / "Join the research programme"
        └─→ SEWA landing page / docs / partnership enquiry
              └─→ Free demo / NIHR research enquiry / NHS procurement query
                    └─→ Platform adoption / study participation / deployment
```

**Key mechanics:**
- **Awareness posts** (Sequence 1, 2) use educational hooks and a "Learn more" CTA to the landing page.
- **Authority posts** (Sequence 3, 4) use evidence and conservative claims to build trust, CTA to the full competitive landscape report.
- **Conversion posts** (Sequence 5) use the research pathway and partnership framing, CTA to the NIHR research programme or demo.
- **Every post ends with one CTA** — no multiple competing CTAs.
- **LinkedIn articles** link directly to the landing page/docs; the landing page drives conversion.
