# SEWA AI — Differentiation at a Glance

**Document Ref:** SEWA-AI-REPORT-CLD-001-1P · Version 1.0 · 20 August 2026

## The One-Line Case

**SEWA AI is the trust-first clinical imaging platform: it doesn't just detect — it knows when it doesn't know, refuses to silently discard, enforces human sign-off that cannot be bypassed, and keeps working when the network fails.**

## The Market

Clinical imaging AI is crowded with well-funded detection tools (Harrison.ai, Aidoc, Qure.ai, Viz.ai, Lunit, Brainomix, DeepHealth/Kheiron) — **none has publicly delivered a full trust architecture**. The NHS already uses AI at scale (≥54% of radiology departments, RCR 2023) and funds it (£123M AI in Health and Care Award) — but adoption still stalls on alert fatigue, opaque single scores, bypassable oversight, cloud-only outages, and thin real-world evidence. **The NHS does not have an AI shortage — it has a trustworthy AI shortage.**

## Why It Matters

**Patients** — time-critical findings (ICH, LVO, PE, pneumothorax, aortic dissection) get a system that escalates when uncertain and never silently discards. **Trusts** — non-bypassable sign-off and a completed DCB0129 safety file de-risk governance; transparent pricing and offline continuity simplify procurement. **Health system** — UK-sovereign, zero-PHI-egress architecture aligns with NHS data-governance expectations.

## The Honest Position

TRL 5 · No UKCA/CE/FDA · No independent clinical validation · No NHS production deployment. Future modules (pathology, mammography, retinal) are roadmap-only with no claims. Internal figures (AUC-ROC 0.961, n=14,200) are **not clinical evidence**. SEWA's challenge is not identity — it is proof: shadow trial → NIHR EME → UKCA Class IIb (Q2 2027).

## The Recommendation

**Race to evidence, publish the safety architecture, and lead with the trustworthy-AI narrative.** The market's winners won on breadth, workflow, or evidence — none won on trust. That is the open lane SEWA was built to own.
