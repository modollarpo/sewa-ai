**What this pack is**
A 14-document evidence and governance dossier supporting a proposed retrospective +
prospective multi-centre clinical validation study across five NHS Trusts
(UHCW, King's College Hospital, UHB, Queen's Hospital Burton, Manchester University
NHS Foundation Trust).

**What this pack is NOT**
It is **not** a claim of proven clinical performance. The investigation is fully
*designed and governed* (powered, pre-specified, risk-managed) but the clinical
validation has **not yet been run** — it is gated on a Chief Investigator,
HRA/MHRA approval, and trained model weights. Internal engineering verification
(Annex 12) used demo weights only and is explicitly non-clinical.
