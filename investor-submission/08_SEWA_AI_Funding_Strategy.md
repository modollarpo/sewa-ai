# SEWA AI — Staged Funding Strategy
**Storegrill Inc Ltd (Company No. 14581073)**
*Seed Round: £650,000 · May 2026*

---

## 1. Capital Strategy Overview
SEWA AI’s funding strategy is designed to de-risk technical, clinical, and regulatory milestones systematically while maintaining capital efficiency. Rather than attempting to fund massive multi-centre clinical trials immediately through equity dilution, SEWA AI employs a staged, non-dilutive capital stack combined with disciplined venture capital.

---

## 2. Stage 1 — £650k Seed Round (Current Raise)
- **Runway**: 18 months.
- **Primary Objectives**:
  - Secure core leadership (CMO, CSO, ML Engineers, Africa Lead).
  - Complete NHS pilot validation (UHCW, King's College, UHB, MFT).
  - Finalise DTAC assessment, G-Cloud 14 listing, and DCB0129 safety documentation.
  - Achieve UKCA Class IIb regulatory readiness preparations.
  - Deploy initial African clinics with offline edge boxes and M-Pesa/Paystack payment integration.
- **Use of Funds**: £403K direct labour (62%), £94K cloud infrastructure (14%), £109K clinical validation (17%), £34K Africa deployment (5%), £38K legal/IP (6%). Match funding: £331,220 from seed investment + early revenue.

---

## 3. Stage 2 — Non-Dilutive & Institutional Grant Funding
Running concurrently with the £650k seed round, SEWA AI leverages UK and international innovation funding mechanisms to subsidise clinical research and validation:
- **NIHR (National Institute for Health and Care Research)**: EME (Efficacy and Mechanism Evaluation) grant applications for clinical evaluation.
- **Innovate UK**: Smart Grants and Biomedical Catalyst competitions for AI safety and sovereign medical device development.
- **HealthTech Research Centres (HTRC)**: Collaborative research partnerships for clinical evaluation and health economics evaluation.
- **NHS Innovation Programmes**: Regional Academic Health Science Network (AHSN) adoption pathways and seed funding.

## 4. Stage 3 — Clinical Investigation Capital & Series A (Year 3)
- **Series A Target**: £5M–£8M in Q1 2027 / Year 3.
- **Objectives**:
  - Scale NHS adoption across 22+ trusts and ICS framework contracts.
  - Expand Sub-Saharan African footprint to 40+ clinics with live federated learning networks.
  - Complete EU MDR CE Mark and FDA 510(k) pre-submissions.
  - Achieve EBITDA positivity (Year 3 H2) on an ARR run-rate of £3.4M.
