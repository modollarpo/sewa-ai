# SEWA AI — Market Research & Sourcing Methodology
*Storegrill Inc Ltd · May 2026*

## 1. Global Medical Imaging AI Market (TAM)
- **Claim**: $22B global medical imaging AI market by 2030.
- **Source Methodology**: Aggregated consensus from primary HealthTech market research reports (Grand View Research, MarketsandMarkets, and McKinsey Healthcare Technology projections).
- **Definition**: Total Addressable Market (TAM) for software, cloud services, and edge hardware supporting diagnostic imaging AI globally.

## 2. Addressable Acute Radiology Market (SAM)
- **Claim**: £4.1B addressable acute radiology triage & reporting market across the UK and Commonwealth/Sub-Saharan Africa.
- **Source Methodology**: Calculated from annual NHS imaging study volume (7.4M studies per year across 220 acute trusts) multiplied by average enterprise software licensing benchmarks per study, plus adjusted addressable public health expenditure in target African urban/regional health systems.
- **Definition**: Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) for acute hospital radiology triage, uncertainty screening, and VLM reporting tools.

## 3. Initial NHS Beachhead (SOM)
- **Claim**: £28M initial NHS acute trust SOM (Year 1–3 ARR targets scaling to £3.4M in Year 3 and £14.6M in Year 5).
- **Source Methodology**: Bottom-up adoption model assuming 2 NHS pilot trusts in Year 1, scaling to 8 trusts in Year 2, 22 trusts in Year 3, 38 trusts in Year 4, and 55 trusts in Year 5, at a blended annual contract value (ACV) of ~£12,990 to £63,000 per trust depending on tier (Starter, Professional, Enterprise).
- **Definition**: Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) capturing realistic acute trust penetration under G-Cloud 14 and ICS framework procurement pathways.

## 4. Sub-Saharan Africa Health Demographics
- **Claim**: 1.4B population, <0.4 radiologists per million population (98%+ deficit against WHO minimum of 20 per million), 25,000+ clinics lacking AI access.
- **Source Methodology**: World Health Organization (WHO) Workforce Statistics, Lancet Commission on Diagnostics, and African Healthcare Federation reports.
