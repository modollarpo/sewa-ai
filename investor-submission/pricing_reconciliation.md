# SEWA AI — Pricing Reconciliation
*Storegrill Inc Ltd · May 2026*

## 1. Pricing Structure Overview
To ensure complete consistency across the public website, financial model, investor pitch deck, and data room documents, SEWA AI maintains a transparent, tier-based B2B SaaS subscription model with usage-based overage expansion.

## 2. Tier Breakdown
- **Africa Edge**: £149/mo (£1,490/yr) — 1,200 scans included. Designed for resource-constrained clinics in Sub-Saharan Africa operating offline with GSM SMS fallback. Overage: £0.49/scan.
- **Starter**: £499/mo (£4,990/yr) — 2,400 scans included. Triage + Uncertainty engines, basic reporting, email alerts, web dashboard. Overage: £1.99/scan.
- **Professional** (Most Popular): £1,299/mo (£12,990/yr) — 15,000 scans included. All 4 engines (Triage, Uncertainty, VLM, Metacognition), VLM structured reports in 8 languages, FHIR R4 + HL7, WhatsApp alerts. Overage: £1.49/scan.
- **Enterprise**: £3,499/mo (£34,990/yr) — 60,000 scans included. Federated learning capability, custom SLA, dedicated Customer Success Manager, optional on-premise deployment. Overage: £0.99/scan.
- **Enterprise+**: Custom pricing for national rollouts, unlimited scans, white-label options, and dedicated SRE support.

## 3. Blended Modelling Convention
In the 5-year financial model (`03_SEWA_AI_Financial_Model.csv`), NHS trust ARR is modelled at a conservative blended average of **£12,990 to £63,000 per trust annually** (Professional to Enterprise tiers), fully reconciled against annual NHS imaging study volumes and overage billing.
