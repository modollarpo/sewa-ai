## 2. Primary Competitor Profiles

### A. Harrison.ai / Annalise AI
- **Positioning**: Comprehensive radiology AI offering multi-finding chest X-ray and CT brain triage.
- **Scope**: Up to 124 chest X-ray findings and comprehensive CT findings.
- **Deployment**: Cloud and enterprise PACS integration.
- **Clinical Evidence**: Mature commercial footprint and clinical validation studies across Australian and international hospitals.
- **SEWA Differentiation**: SEWA combines multi-condition triage with explicit epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty decomposition and a dedicated 9-layer metacognitive safety gate before clinical alert dispatch.

### B. Aidoc
- **Positioning**: Enterprise clinical AI operating system (aiOS) powered by the CARE foundation model.
- **Scope**: Multi-specialty acute care triage (stroke, PE, ICH, rib fractures, spine fractures, etc.) with 35+ European-approved solutions.
- **Deployment**: Enterprise clinical infrastructure with robust governance and monitoring.
- **SEWA Differentiation**: SEWA integrates VLM structured reporting directly with an offline-first edge architecture and differential-privacy federated learning, specifically tailored for NHS and Sub-Saharan African deployments.

### C. Viz.ai
- **Positioning**: AI care pathways and care coordination platform connecting multi-disciplinary clinical teams.
- **Scope**: 50+ AI care pathways across neurovascular, cardiovascular, and pulmonary care; deployed in 2,000+ hospitals globally.
- **Deployment**: Enterprise clinical platform with ISO/IEC 42001 AI governance certification.
- **SEWA Differentiation**: While Viz excels at care coordination and workflow orchestration, SEWA provides a dedicated safety-assurance layer focusing on model uncertainty quantification, hallucination screening, and sovereign on-premise/edge execution.

### D. Qure.ai
- **Positioning**: Global imaging AI platform focused on resource-constrained deployment (qXR, qER, qCT).
- **Scope**: Deployed in 90+ countries across 3,100+ sites (10.7m+ scans processed); strong footprint in TB and chest radiography.
- **Deployment**: Cloud or on-premise options with pre-filled reporting assistance.
- **SEWA Differentiation**: Qure represents the key global benchmark for deployable imaging AI in emerging markets; SEWA builds upon this by introducing multi-engine orchestration, explicit uncertainty decomposition, and a metacognition safety gate.
